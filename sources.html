WORDS THAT HAVE TRAVELLED THROUGH TIME

Classic words, treated with care.

This library contains ten English excerpts from each of six classic works. The texts come from Project Gutenberg, whose catalogue lists the original English works as public domain in the United States. Each practice passage names its chapter and links to the source eBook. The excerpts are not continuous and do not represent complete chapters.

Prepared for typing practice

The original wording is preserved, with longer paragraphs divided at sentence boundaries. Curly quotation marks become straight quotes, long dashes become two hyphens, and repeated whitespace becomes a single space. Page numbers, footnote markers and formatting such as italics are removed from the practice text. Illustrations, modern translations and editorial commentary are not included.

Some passages contain older spellings or usages. These are retained rather than rewritten in modern English.
