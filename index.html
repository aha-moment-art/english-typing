MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

A little time for English, every day.

  1. Choose a text and start typing directly on the passage. On a phone, tap the passage or Start typing to open the keyboard.
  2. Correct letters turn green. A wrong letter flashes red, then only that character needs another try. All preceding correct letters and your accuracy history are preserved. Backspace removes the previous character.
  3. A completed passage advances automatically after a short pause. If you pause or leave the page, use Continue or Enter when ready. You can also jump between passages.

The timer starts with your first character and pauses when you select Pause, press Escape, or leave the practice area or page. WPM counts every five correct characters as one word. Accuracy includes all your typing attempts, so corrections do not erase earlier mistakes.

Pasting is disabled during practice. Use straight quotation marks and type long dashes as two hyphens: --.

Progress stays in this browser and is never uploaded. Clearing browser data removes your progress; it does not sync between devices.
